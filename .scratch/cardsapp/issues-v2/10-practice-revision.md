# 10 - Practice: Revision Mode

**What to build:**
- **Revision Mode Logic:** AI traverses all `Useful Expressions` in the user's DB. AI finds suitable words to replace into the user's text.
- **Highlight & Tooltip:** Replaced words are highlighted. Clicking a highlight opens a tooltip/menu.
- **Tooltip Content:** Shows front (Chinese/English) and back (English explanation) from DB.
- **+1 Use Count:** Tooltip contains a `+1` button specifically for `Use Count` (not Revision Count). Clicking updates the DB. Clicking elsewhere dismisses.
